Battery Circular System

How lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite and copper move through the EV battery value chain, and the five loops that bring them back: intensive use, life extension, second life, end-of-life recycling and manufacturing scrap. Click any box, numbered loop or arrow for details and linked sources.

Figure 1 · Layer 1 linear chain + Layer 2 recycling

From a linear chain to a circular system

The top row is today’s linear chain. After use, batteries are collected and assessed, then routed to life extension, second life or recycling. Recycling turns batteries and factory scrap into battery-grade materials that re-enter the chain at refining or cell production.
